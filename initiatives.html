Initiatives & Programmes.

The national programmes IIC IIT Bombay coordinates from the Ministry of Education, and the initiatives the council is building for students at IIT Bombay.

National programmes.

Programme of the Year · Central & South Asia

HULT Prize

World's largest student competition for social impact

75+ startups at Nationals 2025-26

IIT Bombay has hosted the HULT Prize India Nationals for two consecutive years, most recently bringing 75+ student ventures and 25 judges onto campus. Teams build companies that solve pressing global problems, with the winning team taking the $1M USD prize forward. Our programme was named Programme of the Year for the Central & South Asia zone.

Visit HULT Prize

Smart India Hackathon

India's largest open innovation hackathon

378 in the campus round

An annual national hackathon organised by MIC and AICTE under the Ministry of Education. IIC runs the campus round — 378 participants at the most recent edition — and carries teams through the national journey with mentorship and prototyping grants. IIT Bombay has taken Grand Finale wins in each of the past three years.

Visit SIH portal

KAPILA

Patent filing reimbursement scheme

Up to ₹5,600 per filing

Kalam Programme for IP Literacy and Awareness, a Ministry of Education scheme that reimburses patent filing costs for students, faculty, and staff. IIC coordinates the workflow with IRCC at IIT Bombay.

Learn about KAPILA

YUKTI

National innovation registry

213 innovations submitted

The national innovation registry from the Ministry of Education, with 213 innovations submitted from IIT Bombay to date. Entries on YUKTI join the national database, eligible for recognition, mentorship, and scheme routing. IIC coordinates BTPs, MTPs, and research outputs.

Visit YUKTI
On campus

What we run here.

Programmes the council runs for students at IIT Bombay, beyond the national mandate.

Rulebook near-final

The Innovation Cup

Innovation as a habit, hostel by hostel

Season-long, inter-hostel

Hostels compete across a season on a points table — 24-hour hackathons, prototype challenges and a season finale, run like the Tech or Cultural GC. Built deliberately to reach the students who never turn up to an entrepreneurship event, and to feed teams into SIH and the HULT Prize.

Running

Founder Interview Series

Learning from the people who built it

Alumni founders & industry leaders

Long-form conversations with alumni founders and industry leaders, published for the campus as interviews and written pieces. Most recently with Ashishkumar Chauhan, MD & CEO of the National Stock Exchange, an IIT Bombay alumnus and the creator of the Nifty index.

Ongoing

Campus Collaborations

Working with the bodies already doing this

Across the institute

IIC works alongside E-Cell, the Institute Technical Council, the Tinkerers' Laboratory, Maker Space and the departments, rather than duplicating them. Joint events, shared mentor pools, and a single route for students who do not yet know which door to knock on.

Built by the council

What we are building.

Self-serve tools and guides, so a student can find their way into the ecosystem without having to ask anyone first.

The Resource Vault

38 verified resources

A searchable directory of funding, competitions, incubators, mentorship, prototyping facilities and IP support — split between what is available on campus and what is open across India. Filter by the stage you are at, from a first idea to a growing company.

In final review

Innovation 101

A student's map of the IIT Bombay ecosystem

15 chapters

A booklet covering SINE, DSSE, E-Cell, the Tinkerers' Laboratory, Maker Space, the Research Park, BETIC, IRCC, GESH and the national programmes — what each one does, who it is for, and how to approach it. Every chapter is being reviewed by the body it describes before publication.

In development

The Innovation Chatbot

Ask a question, get pointed to the right door

Grounded in verified sources

A conversational assistant for student questions about courses, labs, facilities, funding and the path from an idea to a company. Built on a curated set of verified IIT Bombay sources so that every answer can be traced back to where it came from.

In development

MOOC for Freshmen

The whole ecosystem, before you need to ask

8 episodes · 5–8 min each

A short video-lecture series walking every incoming student through the innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystem, one entity per episode — from IIC and DSSE through SINE, the Tinkerers' Laboratory, KAPILA and YUKTI, to going from an idea to a funded startup. Self-paced and replayable.

Beyond campus

Reaching schools and smaller colleges.

Part of the IIC mandate is handholding institutions that are building their own innovation programmes, and reaching students long before they arrive at a place like IIT Bombay.

In production

The Innovation Magazine

Taking innovation to school students

Classes 9 to 12

A magazine for school students across the Mumbai suburban region — innovation spotlights, founder stories, trends and explainers, and windows into the IIT Bombay ecosystem. Researched and written by a student editorial team within the council.

Running

Innovation Ambassador & ATL

Taking the ecosystem to where students already are

Schools & smaller colleges

Handholding schools, smaller colleges and certified faculty as they build innovation programmes of their own, through workshops, mentor sessions and industrial visits. One of the three things the Ministry asks every IIC to do, alongside reporting and closing gaps on its own campus.
