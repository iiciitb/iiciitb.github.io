Innovation Ecosystem at IIT Bombay.

The bodies, labs, and centres that support innovation on campus. IIC sits across them and connects students to the right one for their stage.

SINE

SINE

IIT Bombay's startup incubator

Society for Innovation and Entrepreneurship. Supports startups with incubation, mentorship, and infrastructure. Has supported 200+ alumni companies across deep-tech, healthcare, fintech, and consumer.

Visit SINE
AS

Aspire

IIT Bombay Research Park

The institute's R&D and innovation park. Physical infrastructure where companies, labs, and academia co-locate to run pilot projects, industry-academic partnerships, and translational research.

Visit Aspire
BETIC

BETIC

Biomedical engineering incubation

Specialised incubator for medical technology, devices, and diagnostics. Supports student and faculty projects through prototyping, regulatory navigation, and clinical validation.

Visit BETIC
DSSE

DSSE

Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship

The school within IIT Bombay focused on entrepreneurship education, research, and community. Powers IIC IIT Bombay.

Visit DSSE
MS

MakerSpace

Fabrication facility

An additional fabrication facility complementary to Tinkerers' Lab. Different tooling, different scheduling, same spirit of making.

TL

Tinkerers' Lab

Prototyping and maker space

Open to students for hardware prototyping, electronics, and rapid iteration. The first stop for many student innovators on campus.

EC

E-Cell

The Entrepreneurship Cell

A student-run body focused on entrepreneurship education and flagship events. Best known for Eureka, one of India's largest student business competitions.

Visit E-Cell
