Campus Treasure Hunt

IIC IIT Bombay · Teams register on the hunt portal

A two-day hunt across the institute. Teams register, receive one hint at a time, and upload a photograph at each location for the coordinating team to verify. Run in place of the conventional orientation, as a way for new students to find their way around the campus.
